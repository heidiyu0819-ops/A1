Introduction

My love for visual aesthetics started early - when I was a kid, I would spend hours drawing with ink and paint, fascinated by how lines and colors could turn a blank paper into a little something or feelings of my own. That early curiocity about beauty and composition laid the foundation for everything I do today.

Working across photography, design, and print, I aim for clarity and intentionality in every project.

High school Art Work

For me, art is a continuous thread—from childhood doodles to professional visuals, it’s all about seeing the extraordinary in the ordinary, and translating that into something others can feel.

Later, with my phone in hand, I grew obsessed with preserving traces of beauty through my camera. This sparked a deep interest in artistic creation—I found that both photography and graphic design were perfect outlets for me to channel and express all my ideas. It was not until last year that, with my own savings, I bought my very first camera.

Each frame explores stillness and the beauty of unplanned moments.

My biggest achievement is turning my long-lasting love for art and colors into a real visual practice.From drawing as a kid to self-funding my first camera, I have built my own creative voice through photography, post-processing, and design.

I have always been someone who expresses emotions through colors and tones.Art, creativity, and visual aesthetics have been deeply rooted in me since I was little.Over time, this love gradually led me to photography—a medium where I can truly translate my feelings into images. Whether I am capturing portraits, events, architecture, landscapes, or still life, I approach each scene with a strong sense of color and mood.After shooting, I take charge of post-processing, retouching, and color grading to tailor a unique style that fits the story and emotion of every photo. To me, photography is not just about taking pictures.
